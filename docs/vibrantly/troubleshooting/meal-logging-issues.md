# Meal Logging Issues

## Meal not saving after I type it

If you typed a meal description and it did not appear in your Meals history:

1. Check that you pressed **Enter** or tapped **Send** to submit — the meal is only logged when you confirm.
2. Refresh the page and check the Meals list.
3. If the meal still did not save, try logging it again via the **Chat** tab using the Log meal flow.

## Nutrition estimates look wrong

Luna estimates nutrition from your description. The accuracy depends on how specific you are.

| Less accurate | More accurate |
|---------------|--------------|
| "Salad" | "Caesar salad with grilled chicken and croutons, about 300g" |
| "Pasta" | "One cup cooked spaghetti with tomato sauce and 100g ground beef" |
| "Chicken" | "200g grilled chicken breast with salt and pepper" |

If Luna's estimate is clearly off, you can ask her to revise it in Chat: "That meal was more like 600 calories — can you update the estimate?"

## Meal type is wrong (Breakfast logged as Lunch, etc.)

Meal type is automatically assigned based on time of logging. If it is wrong, tap the meal in your history and edit the meal type.

## Luna's chat panel does not open when I tap "Log meal"

1. Try a hard refresh of the page.
2. If you are on mobile, close and reopen the app.
3. If the issue persists, log the meal from the **Today** view meal bar instead — type directly into the input at the top of the page.

## I logged a meal but it is not showing in Today view

The Today view shows only your most recent meals. If you logged a meal earlier in the day, scroll down in the **From Your Meals** section or tap **See all** to go to the full Meals history.
