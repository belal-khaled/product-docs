# Deleting Your Account

Deleting your account permanently removes all your data from Vibrantly — lab reports, meal logs, memories, pages, and chat history. This action cannot be undone.

## Before you delete

Consider whether you want to:

- **Download your lab reports** — go to **Lab reports** and download any files you want to keep
- **Export a page** — open any Page you want to keep and download or export the HTML before deleting

## How to delete your account

1. Go to **Settings** from the sidebar.
2. Open the **Account** tab.
3. Find the **Delete account** option and follow the confirmation steps.

You will be asked to confirm your decision. Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted.

## After deletion

- You will be signed out immediately
- Your email address will be released and can be used to create a new Vibrantly account
- Deleted data cannot be recovered

If you are having trouble with the product rather than wanting to leave permanently, our [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting/index.md) guides may help, or contact Vibrantly support.
