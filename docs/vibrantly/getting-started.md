# Getting Started with Vibrantly

Vibrantly is your personal health intelligence platform. It connects your lab results, meals, and daily signals to give you a clear, plain-English picture of your health — powered by Luna, your AI health companion.

![Vibrantly welcome screen](img/welcome.png)

## Creating Your Account

1. Open the Vibrantly app or visit [staging.vibrantly.com](https://staging.vibrantly.com).
2. Enter your email address and tap **Continue**.
3. Enter your password and tap **Sign in**.
   - Alternatively, use **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple** for one-tap access.
4. On first sign-in, you will be taken through a short setup to personalize Luna for your health goals.

## Your First Login

After signing in, you land on the **Today** dashboard. This is your home base — it shows a personalised greeting, a summary of your recent meals, and any active body signal insights.

The left sidebar (desktop) or bottom bar (mobile) gives you access to all sections:

| Section | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Today** | Daily dashboard — meal entry, AI insights, body signal summary |
| **Meals** | Log meals and view your full nutrition history |
| **Body** | Your biomarkers from lab reports, explained in plain language |
| **Lab Reports** | Upload and manage your health reports |
| **Chat** | Have a free-form conversation with Luna |
| **Pages** | AI-generated health summaries and personal notes |
| **My AI** | Customize Luna's personality and behavior |
| **Skills** | Browse and use specialized AI tools |
| **Memories** | See what Luna has learned about you |
