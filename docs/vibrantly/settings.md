# Settings & Profile

## Your Profile

Your profile is accessible via your initials or avatar in the top-right corner of the sidebar. It shows:

- **Display name** — used by Luna in greetings and throughout the app
- **Member status** — your current plan tier
- **Theme** — Light / Dark mode toggle (available on desktop)

## Account Management

To manage your account (email, password, connected sign-in methods, billing), navigate to **Settings** from the sidebar (or tap **More** on the mobile bottom bar).

## Privacy & Data

- Lab report data is encrypted at rest and de-identified before any AI analysis runs.
- Memories are built automatically from your Chat conversations. You can ask Luna to discard a specific memory via Chat.
- Meal logs and lab reports are private to your account. Pages you create can be set to **Private**, **Private Link** (shareable by URL), or **Public**.

## Pages

**Pages** (`/dashboard/pages`) are AI-generated health documents — for example, a plain-English interpretation of a lab report, or a personalized nutrition blueprint. They are created by Luna when you ask for a report summary.

Each page has a visibility setting:

| Visibility | Access |
|-----------|--------|
| **Private** | Only you |
| **Private Link** | Anyone with the link |
| **Public** | Discoverable by others |

You can view and manage all your pages from the Pages section.
