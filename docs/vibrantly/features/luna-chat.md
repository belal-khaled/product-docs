# Chat with Luna

**Luna** is Vibrantly's built-in AI health companion. Every response is grounded in your actual health data — she is not a general-purpose chatbot.

![Chat with Luna](../img/chat.png)

## Starting a conversation

Go to **Chat** in the sidebar to start a free-form conversation. Luna can:

- Answer questions about your biomarkers in plain English
- Help you understand the nutritional impact of what you ate
- Give coaching check-ins on your health goals (e.g. half-marathon training, blood sugar management)
- Analyze a meal photo or restaurant menu

Your conversation history is stored and accessible from the sidebar — Luna carries context across sessions.

## What Luna knows

Luna has access to everything you've shared with Vibrantly:

| Data | How Luna uses it |
|------|----------------|
| Lab reports | Explains your biomarkers, flags patterns, answers "what does this mean?" |
| Meal logs | Connects eating patterns to your lab results |
| Goals | Frames responses around what you're working toward |
| Memories | Carries context from past conversations without you having to repeat yourself |
| Skills | Can invoke specialized tools (Food Analyzer, Health Check-in) within a conversation |

## Asking good questions

Luna responds best to specific, contextual questions:

- "My ferritin came back low — what foods would help?"
- "I had a big pasta dinner last night — how does that affect my glucose?"
- "Give me a weekly check-in on my blood sugar goals"

For general conversation and coaching, Luna's default archetype is **Sage** — thoughtful, question-led, and never prescriptive. You can change this in [Companion Settings](companion-settings.md).

## Limits

Luna will never give medical, legal, or financial advice. If you ask something that falls outside her boundaries, she will acknowledge it and direct you to a qualified professional.
