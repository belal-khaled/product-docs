# Today View

The **Today** dashboard is the first thing you see when you open Vibrantly. It combines your daily meal activity, a personalized AI insight, and a snapshot of your body signals.

![Today dashboard](../img/today.png)

## Daily greeting and insight

At the top, Luna greets you by name and surfaces the most relevant health insight for the day — based on your lab results, recent meals, and stated goals.

## Logging a meal

Type a description of what you ate in the **Log a meal** bar at the top of the page (e.g. "Fried chicken sandwich and black coffee"). Luna will:

1. Identify the meal and estimate its nutritional breakdown (calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber)
2. Relate it to your current health goals
3. Show you what the meal means for your body

You can also log meals by photo — open **Chat** and use the **Food Analyzer** skill.

## Today's Insight card

Below the meal bar, Luna shows a **Today's Insight** card — a concise observation based on patterns in your data. This may highlight a trend across your recent meals, a connection between a biomarker and your eating habits, or a forming pattern worth watching.

## Meals summary

![Meals history](../img/meals.png)

The **From Your Meals** section shows your most recently logged meals with time, meal type, macro breakdown, and Luna's interpretation. Tap **See all** to go to the full [Meals](meals.md) view.

## Body signals summary

The bottom of Today shows a **Body Signals** snapshot — calories tracked today and key biomarker flags from your latest lab report. Tap any item to go to the full [Body Signals](body-signals.md) view.
