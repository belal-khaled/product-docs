# Body Signals

The **Body** view shows your complete biomarker picture — categorized, annotated, and prioritized by Luna based on your uploaded lab reports.

![Body view — biomarkers explained in plain language](../img/body.png)

## Biomarker summary

At the top, Luna gives a natural-language summary: what looks strong, what caught her attention, and why.

## Status labels

| Label | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Excellent** | Within the optimal range |
| **Borderline** | On the edge — worth monitoring |
| **Needs attention** | Outside reference range; action likely needed |
| **Missing** | Not found in your uploaded reports |

Use the filter buttons (**All / Excellent / Borderline / Needs attention / Missing**) to focus on what needs your attention.

## Biomarker categories

Vibrantly groups your biomarkers by body system:

- **Hormonal** — Cortisol, Testosterone, Thyroid, DHEA, Estradiol
- **Cardiometabolic** — Cholesterol (LDL, HDL), Triglycerides, Blood Sugar, Inflammation
- **Liver Function** — ALT, AST, GGT, Bilirubin
- **Kidney Function** — Creatinine, Blood Urea
- **Complete Blood Count** — Red cells, White cells, Hemoglobin, Platelets
- **Vitamins & Minerals** — Vitamin D, B12, Folic Acid, Iron, Zinc, Magnesium
- **Inflammation** — CRP (C-Reactive Protein)
- **Autoimmune** — Anti-CCP, RA Factor
- **Pancreatic Function** — Amylase, Lipase

## Biological age

Based on your latest panel, Vibrantly estimates a **biological age** — a composite signal of how your body is performing relative to your chronological age. The number updates each time you upload a new report.

## Connection to meals

Luna cross-references your biomarkers with your meal history. If your glucose patterns show variability, she flags relevant meals in the Today view and explains the connection.

If a biomarker is marked **Missing**, see [Lab Report Issues](../troubleshooting/lab-report-issues.md).
