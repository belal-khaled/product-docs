# Meals

Vibrantly tracks every meal you log and connects your eating patterns to your lab results. Logging is conversational — there are no forms to fill out.

## Meal history

![Meals list](../img/meals-list.png)

Go to **Meals** in the sidebar to see your full meal log. Each entry shows:

- Date and time
- Meal type (Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner / Snack)
- Macronutrient breakdown (protein, fiber, carbs, fat, calories)
- Luna's interpretation of how the meal fits your goals

## Logging a meal

### Text (from Today view)

Type a description in the **Log a meal** bar on the Today dashboard — e.g. "Grilled salmon with roasted vegetables and water". Luna parses the description and estimates nutrition automatically.

### Chat logging (text, photo, or voice)

![Meal logging via Luna chat](../img/meals-log-dialog.png)

Tap **Log meal** from the Meals page to open Luna's meal logging panel. This is a chat interface — describe what you ate, upload a photo of the meal, or use voice input. Luna responds with a nutritional breakdown and contextual analysis.

### Food Analyzer skill

Open **Chat** and invoke the **Food Analyzer** skill for a structured meal analysis — useful for restaurant meals, menu photos, or detailed nutritional breakdowns.

## How Luna analyzes meals

For each logged meal, Luna:

1. Estimates macronutrients based on the description or photo
2. Checks the meal against your active health goals (e.g. blood sugar management, protein targets)
3. Cross-references with your latest biomarkers — if glucose variability is flagged in your lab report, she will note relevant meals

## Meal type classification

Meals are automatically classified as Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or Snack based on the time of logging and context from your description. You can override the classification in the meal log.
