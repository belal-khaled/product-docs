# Billing & Plans

Your Vibrantly subscription is managed through your account settings.

## Viewing your plan

Your current plan tier is shown next to your name in the sidebar (e.g. **Member**). For full plan details — including your renewal date and what's included — go to **Settings > Account**.

## Upgrading or changing your plan

To upgrade your plan or explore available tiers, contact Vibrantly support or visit the billing section within the app on your device. Plan options and pricing are managed through the platform and may vary by region.

## Cancellation

To cancel your subscription, go to **Settings > Account** and follow the cancellation steps, or contact Vibrantly support. Cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period — you retain access until then.

## Refunds

Refund eligibility depends on your subscription type and region. Contact Vibrantly support with your account email to request a refund review.

## Need help?

If you have a billing question that is not covered here, contact Vibrantly support directly from within the app or at [vibrantly.com](https://vibrantly.com).
