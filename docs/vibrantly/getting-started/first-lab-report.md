# Uploading Your First Lab Report

Uploading a lab report is the most important first action in Vibrantly — it gives Luna the data she needs to personalize everything else.

![Lab reports — upload and manage your health files](../img/my-data.png)

## Supported formats

| Format | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| PDF | Most lab reports from clinics and hospitals |
| CSV | Data exports from health platforms |
| Plain text | Copied values pasted into a text file |
| Photo | A photo of a printed or handwritten report |

## How to upload

1. From the sidebar, tap **Lab reports**.
2. Click **Upload** or drag your file into the upload zone.
3. Vibrantly processes your report using 3 independent AI models that cross-check each other's findings. This takes under 60 seconds.
4. When complete, tap **Body** in the sidebar to view your extracted biomarkers.

Your uploaded reports are listed on the Lab reports page with their file name, date, and status (**Saved** or **Analyzed**).

## Privacy

Your report data is encrypted at rest. Before AI analysis runs, all personal identifiers are removed — the models see your biomarker values, not your name or contact details. Vibrantly stores data in compliance with HIPAA standards.

## What happens after upload

Once your first report is processed, Luna will:

- Surface a **body signals summary** on your Today dashboard
- Generate a plain-English explanation of your key biomarkers in **Body**
- Begin cross-referencing your meals with your biomarker patterns

If a biomarker is missing or the report did not parse correctly, see [Lab Report Issues](../troubleshooting/lab-report-issues.md).
