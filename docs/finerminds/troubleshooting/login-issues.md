# Login Issues

## Forgot your password

1. Go to [finerminds.com](https://www.finerminds.com) and click **Log in**
2. Click **Forgot password?**
3. Enter your email address and click **Send reset link**
4. Check your inbox — the link arrives within a few minutes
5. Check your spam/junk folder if it doesn't appear

The reset link expires after 24 hours. If it has expired, request a new one.

## Reset email not arriving

- Confirm you are using the email address registered to your account
- Check spam, junk, and promotions folders
- Add `noreply@finerminds.com` (or `@mindvalley.com`) to your contacts and request another reset

## Google sign-in not working

- Make sure you are selecting the same Google account used when you first signed up
- If your browser blocks third-party cookies, allow them for `finerminds.com` or try a different browser
- Try signing in via an incognito/private window to rule out a cached session

## Account not found

If you receive "account not found" when entering your email, you may not yet have a Finerminds account. Go to [finerminds.com](https://www.finerminds.com) and click **Sign up**.

## Stuck on a loading screen after login

1. Clear your browser cache and cookies for finerminds.com
2. Hard-refresh (`Ctrl+Shift+R` / `Cmd+Shift+R`)
3. Try a different browser or device
