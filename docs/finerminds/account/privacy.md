# Privacy & Data

Go to **Settings → Privacy & data** to review your privacy options and access legal documents.

## What Finerminds stores

Finerminds stores the information you provide in your profile — name, photo, location, interests, profession, and social links — along with your activity such as messages, event RSVPs, and group memberships.

Your email address is used only for sign-in and account-related notifications. It is not shown on your public profile.

## What other members can see

| Information | Visible to |
|-------------|-----------|
| Name, avatar, job title | All members |
| Home city, languages, interests | All members |
| About me, social links | All members |
| Email address | Nobody (private) |
| Direct messages | Only the participants |

## Legal

From **Settings → Privacy & data**, you can read:

- **Terms of use** — the rules governing your use of Finerminds
- **Privacy policy** — how your data is collected, stored, and used

## Deleting your account

To request account deletion, contact Mindvalley support. All your profile data, messages, and activity will be permanently removed.
