# Signing In

Go to [finerminds.com](https://www.finerminds.com) and click **Log in**.

## Sign-in methods

- **Google** — click **Continue with Google** and choose your account
- **Email and password** — enter the email address linked to your account

## Resetting your password

1. On the sign-in screen, click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email address and click **Send reset link**
3. Open the email and click the link — it expires after 24 hours
4. Choose a new password and confirm it

Alternatively, go to **Settings → My account** and click **Reset password** while logged in.

## Changing your username

Go to **Settings → My account**. Under **Username**, click **Change** and enter the @handle you want. Usernames must be unique across Finerminds.

## Changing your display name

Go to **Settings → Edit profile** and update the **First name** and **Last name** fields.

## Signing out

Go to **Settings** and click **Log out** at the bottom of the menu.
