# Getting Started

Get up and running on Finerminds in three steps.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- :material-account-plus: **Create your account**

    Sign up with your email or an existing Mindvalley account.

    [→ Creating your account](creating-account.md)

- :material-card-account-details: **Set up your profile**

    Add a photo, your location, interests, and profession so others can find and connect with you.

    [→ Your profile and home page](home-page.md)

- :material-account-group: **Join a network**

    Networks are where the community lives. Find one that fits your team or interests and join.

    [→ Networks](../features/networks.md)

</div>
