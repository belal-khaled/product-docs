# Your Profile and Home Page

## Home page

When you log in, Finerminds takes you to the **Networks** page — the main hub for everything happening in your community. From here you can navigate to People, Chats, and Events using the sidebar.

![Networks home](../img/networks.png)

## Your profile

Your profile is your identity on Finerminds. Other members see it when they browse People or visit your network.

![Your profile](../img/profile.png)

### What your profile shows

| Field | Where it appears |
|-------|-----------------|
| Name and avatar | Everywhere — people directory, chat, networks |
| Job title and company | People directory and profile page |
| Home city | People directory filters |
| Interests | Recommended connections |
| About me | Profile page |
| Social links | Profile page |

### Editing your profile

Go to **Settings → Edit profile** to update any of these fields. Changes are saved when you click **Save**.

See [Editing your profile](../account/editing-profile.md) for full details.

## Navigation

The sidebar gives you access to all main sections:

| Sidebar item | What it opens |
|---|---|
| All networks | Networks listing |
| All people | Member directory |
| All chats | Your conversations |
| All events | Events calendar |
