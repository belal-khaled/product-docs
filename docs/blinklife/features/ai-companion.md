# AI Companion (My AI)

**My AI** is where you configure Sandra — her personality, communication style, name, and how she understands you. Go to **My AI** in the sidebar.

![My AI — Sandra personality settings](../img/my-ai.png)

## Tabs in My AI

| Tab | What it controls |
|-----|----------------|
| **Personality** | Archetype, communication rules, anti-patterns |
| **Profile** | Companion name |
| **My Human** | Information about you that Sandra should know |
| **Structured / Soul.md** | Advanced view of Sandra's core instructions |

## Personality archetypes

Choose an archetype to set Sandra's overall communication tone. The current default is **Friend**.

**Friend** means Sandra:
- Talks like someone who genuinely knows and likes you — casual, warm, real
- Has opinions and shares them honestly — she does not just validate
- Matches your energy — excited when you are, listens first when you are venting
- Uses humor naturally, not forced
- References past conversations to show she remembers

Other archetypes shift the default tone (e.g. more structured, more formal, more Socratic). Select the one that fits how you want to work with Sandra.

## Editing communication rules

Within the Personality tab, you can add, remove, or rewrite the specific rules that govern how Sandra communicates. Changes take effect immediately on the next message.

## Renaming Sandra

Go to **My AI → Profile** and edit the companion name field. The name change applies across the entire interface immediately (up to a set character limit).

## My Human tab

Tell Sandra about yourself — your role, your goals, your working style. The more she knows, the more relevant her responses become. This context supplements what she learns from your conversations and memories.

## Soul.md

An advanced view of Sandra's full instruction set in a structured format. This is for users who want to inspect or understand the complete personality configuration.
