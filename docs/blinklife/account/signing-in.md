# Signing In

Go to [blinklife.com](https://www.blinklife.com) and click **Sign in**.

## Signing in with email

BlinkLife uses a two-step login:

1. Enter your **Email address** and click **Continue**.
2. Enter your **Password** and click **Continue** again.

## Signing in with Google

On the login screen, click **Sign in with Google**. If you created your account with Google, this is the only sign-in method you need — no password required.

## Staying signed in

BlinkLife keeps you signed in on trusted devices. If you are signed out unexpectedly, simply sign in again.

## Having trouble?

- Forgot your password → [Password Reset](password-reset.md)
- "Incorrect email or password" → confirm the email is the one you signed up with, then reset your password
- Google SSO not working → see [Login Issues](../troubleshooting/login-issues.md)
