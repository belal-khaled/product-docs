# Deleting Your Account

Deleting your BlinkLife account is permanent. All your data — notes, tasks, pages, chats, memories, diary entries — will be erased and cannot be recovered.

## Before you delete

Consider exporting or copying anything you want to keep:
- Export memories from **Memories → Export**
- Copy key notes or pages manually

## How to delete your account

1. Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar to open the profile menu.
2. Select **Settings**.
3. Navigate to the **Account** tab.
4. Scroll to the bottom and click **Delete account**.
5. Confirm your decision in the dialog that appears.

Account deletion takes effect immediately.

## After deletion

- You will be signed out and cannot sign back in with this email
- Any active subscription is cancelled — you will not be billed further
- Your data is removed from BlinkLife's servers in accordance with their privacy policy

If you signed up via Google, deleting the BlinkLife account does not affect your Google account.
