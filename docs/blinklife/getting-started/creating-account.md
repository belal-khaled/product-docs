# Creating Your Account

Go to [blinklife.com](https://www.blinklife.com) and click **Sign in** to create a new account or log in.

## Signing up with email

1. On the welcome screen, enter your **Email address** and click **Continue**.
2. If this is a new account, you will be prompted to set a **Password**.
3. Complete any onboarding steps — BlinkLife will introduce you to Sandra and ask a few setup questions.

## Signing up with Google

Below the email field, click **Sign in with Google** to create your account using your Google credentials. No separate password is required.

## Onboarding

After signup, BlinkLife walks you through:

1. Meeting Sandra — your AI companion
2. Setting Sandra's personality (archetype, communication style)
3. Naming Sandra if you prefer a different name

All of these settings can be changed later from [My AI](../features/ai-companion.md).

## Already have an account?

Go to [Signing In](../account/signing-in.md) if you already have an account and need help logging in.
