# Getting Started with BlinkLife

BlinkLife is an AI companion platform built around Sandra — an AI that learns about you, manages your workspace, and helps you think through your day. These guides walk you through setting up and getting your bearings.

## In this section

- [Creating Your Account](creating-account.md) — sign up with email or Google
- [Personalising Your Companion](personalising-companion.md) — set Sandra's personality, name, and communication style
- [Your Home Page](home-page.md) — understand your daily briefing and what Sandra watches for you
