# Login Issues

## "Incorrect email or password"

- Confirm you are using the email address you signed up with.
- Passwords are case-sensitive — check Caps Lock.
- If you signed up with Google SSO, there is no BlinkLife password — use **Sign in with Google** instead.
- [Reset your password](../account/password-reset.md) if you are unsure.

## Google Sign-In is not working

**Symptom:** Clicking **Sign in with Google** opens the Google prompt but returns an error.

**Try:**
1. Ensure you are selecting the correct Google account (the one used to sign up).
2. Clear your browser cookies and cache, then try again.
3. Try a different browser or an incognito window.
4. If Google shows "Access blocked", the OAuth session may have expired — try signing out of Google and back in before retrying.

## Account says it doesn't exist

If you receive "No account found for this email", you may:
- Have signed up with a different email — check for other email addresses you own.
- Have signed up with Google SSO but are now trying email login — try **Sign in with Google**.
- Not yet have an account — go to [blinklife.com](https://www.blinklife.com) and select **Sign up**.

## Locked out / can't access account email

If you no longer have access to the email address associated with your account, contact BlinkLife support via the **Need help?** link on the login screen.

## Session expires immediately after login

This can happen if your browser blocks third-party cookies. Allow cookies for `blinklife.com` in your browser settings, or try a browser with standard cookie settings.
