# Calendar Sync Issues

## Events not appearing in BlinkLife

**Symptom:** You added an event in Google Calendar but it is not showing in BlinkLife's Calendar tab.

**Try:**
1. Wait a few minutes — sync is not instant.
2. Refresh the BlinkLife page.
3. Go to **Settings → Integrations** and confirm Google Calendar shows as **Connected**.
4. Disconnect and reconnect Google Calendar to force a fresh sync.

## Events from the wrong calendar are showing

BlinkLife syncs your **primary** Google Calendar. If your events are in a secondary calendar or a shared calendar, they may not appear.

To check: in Google Calendar, verify the event is on the calendar marked "Primary" (usually your email address).

## Daily briefing does not include today's events

Sandra's Morning Briefing routine reads your calendar at the time the routine runs. If you added an event after the briefing ran, it will not be in that day's briefing — but you can ask Sandra directly: "What's on my calendar today?"

## Past events disappeared

BlinkLife's Calendar shows a rolling window of events. Very old events may not be visible by default. Use the calendar date picker to navigate to a specific date.

## Disconnecting and reconnecting

If sync is persistently broken:
1. **Settings → Integrations → Disconnect** Google Calendar.
2. Wait 30 seconds.
3. **Connect** Google Calendar again and re-approve permissions.
