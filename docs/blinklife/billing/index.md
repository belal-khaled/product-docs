# Billing & Credits

Manage your BlinkLife subscription and credit balance from within the app.

## Accessing billing

1. Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Select **Settings**.
3. Navigate to the **Billing** tab.

## Subscription plans

BlinkLife offers paid plans with different levels of access to Sandra's capabilities and storage. Details of current plans and pricing are shown in the **Billing** tab inside the app.

## AI Credits

Some actions — particularly intensive or long Chat sessions — consume AI credits. Your current credit balance is displayed in **Settings → Billing**. Credits replenish on your billing cycle.

## Cancelling your subscription

To cancel, go to **Settings → Billing** and select the cancellation option. Your subscription remains active until the end of the paid period, after which your account moves to the free tier.

## Refunds and billing issues

For billing disputes or refund requests, contact BlinkLife support via the **Help** link inside the app.
