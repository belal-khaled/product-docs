# Telegram

Connect Telegram to send messages to Sandra without opening BlinkLife. Your conversation syncs back to the Chat tab.

## Connecting Telegram

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations**.
2. Click **Connect** next to Telegram.
3. Follow the instructions to link your Telegram account (typically by starting a conversation with the BlinkLife bot from the Telegram app).

## Using Sandra via Telegram

Once connected, message the BlinkLife bot in Telegram as you would in the Chat tab:
- Ask questions, capture thoughts, add tasks
- Sandra replies in Telegram and the conversation appears in your BlinkLife Chat history

Telegram is useful for capturing ideas on the go without switching apps.

## Limitations

- You cannot access Notes, Pages, or Calendar directly from Telegram — those require the BlinkLife app
- Voice messages sent in Telegram do not trigger Listener; use the in-app mic for that

## Disconnecting

Go to **Settings → Integrations** and click **Disconnect** next to Telegram.
