# Google Calendar

Connect Google Calendar to bring your schedule into BlinkLife. Your events appear in the **Calendar** tab and are included in Sandra's **Daily Briefing** routine each morning.

## Connecting Google Calendar

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations**.
2. Click **Connect** next to Google Calendar.
3. Sign in with the Google account that holds your calendar.
4. Approve the permissions BlinkLife requests.

Once connected, events from your primary Google Calendar sync into BlinkLife automatically.

## What syncs

- All events from your primary Google Calendar
- Event title, time, date, and description
- Recurring events

BlinkLife reads your calendar — it does not write events back to Google Calendar from BlinkLife.

## Calendar in the daily briefing

Sandra's **Morning Briefing** routine includes today's calendar events by default. If you want to adjust what Sandra says about them, edit the Morning Briefing routine in [Pulse → Routines](../features/pulse.md).

## Disconnecting

Go to **Settings → Integrations** and click **Disconnect** next to Google Calendar. Your past calendar events remain in BlinkLife's Calendar view but will not update further.
