# Integrations

Connect BlinkLife with tools you already use to bring your schedule, messages, and documents into one place.

## Available integrations

- [Google Calendar](google-calendar.md) — sync your events into BlinkLife's Calendar and your daily briefing
- [Gmail](gmail.md) — surface important emails in Sandra's daily summary
- [Telegram](telegram.md) — message Sandra directly from Telegram without opening the app
- [Google Docs](google-docs.md) — attach and reference Google documents inside your notes and pages

## Coming soon

**Bridge** and **WhatsApp** integrations are in development and not yet available to all users. They will appear in **Settings → Integrations** when enabled for your account.

## Managing integrations

All integrations are managed from **Settings → Integrations** (click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then select **Settings**).
