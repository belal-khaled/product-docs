# Gmail

Connect Gmail to let Sandra surface important emails in your daily briefing and respond to email-related questions in Chat.

## Connecting Gmail

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations**.
2. Click **Connect** next to Gmail.
3. Sign in with your Google account and approve the requested permissions.

## What Sandra can do with Gmail

- Include a summary of unread or priority emails in your **Morning Briefing**
- Answer questions like "Do I have any emails from [name] this week?" in Chat
- Flag emails you might have missed based on your stated priorities

BlinkLife does not send emails on your behalf.

## Privacy

BlinkLife reads email metadata and content only to generate summaries within Sandra's context. Email content is not stored or used to train models.

## Disconnecting

Go to **Settings → Integrations** and click **Disconnect** next to Gmail.
