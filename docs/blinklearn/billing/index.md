# Billing & Plans

BlinkLearn is available on subscription plans that unlock different levels of content and AI features. Plan details and pricing are shown inside the app under your account settings.

## Managing your subscription

To view or change your plan, go to your [profile settings](../account/profile-settings.md) and look for the **Subscription** section.

## Cancelling

You can cancel your subscription at any time. You'll retain access to your plan's features until the end of your current billing period.

## Questions about billing?

For billing-specific questions, contact the support team directly via the in-app support option.
