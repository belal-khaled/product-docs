# My AI

**My AI** is where you personalise your AI companion Sandra — her name, voice, personality, and how she understands you. Open it from the sidebar.

![My AI — Personality tab](../img/my-ai.png)

My AI has three tabs: **Personality**, **Profile**, and **My Human**.

---

## Personality tab

The Personality tab controls how Sandra behaves in conversation. It has two modes:

- **Structured** — set a named archetype (e.g. Friend, Coach, Mentor) that drives Sandra's communication style
- **Soul.md** — write free-form instructions in markdown for full control over her tone and behaviour

### Archetype

Choose one of the preset archetypes as Sandra's baseline character. Each archetype comes with a set of default communication rules. Click **Change** to switch to a different archetype.

### How Sandra Communicates

A list of editable rules that govern Sandra's style — things like tone, humour, and how she handles feedback. Rules are tagged with the archetype they came from. You can add custom rules with **+ Add** or delete any rule individually.

### Learned Adaptations

Sandra picks up patterns from your conversations automatically and stores them here. These are distinct from rules you set manually — they reflect how Sandra has naturally adapted to you over time.

### Boundaries

Rules about what Sandra won't do, regardless of archetype. Use this to set hard limits on topics or behaviour.

### Custom Instructions

Free-form text that Sandra always considers, regardless of the active archetype. Use this for context that doesn't fit into a specific rule.

---

## Profile tab

The Profile tab controls Sandra's identity and voice.

### Identity

Set Sandra's name and avatar. The name appears throughout the app and in chat. Change it to anything that feels right for you.

### Voice

Choose how Sandra sounds in voice mode. Six options are available:

| Voice | Style |
|-------|-------|
| Zara | Confident and direct |
| Mia | Bright and articulate |
| Lena | Gentle and conversational |
| Kai | Energetic and expressive |
| Leo | Deep and unhurried |
| Theo | Grounded and clear |

Click the play button next to any voice to preview it, then click **Save Changes**.

---

## My Human tab

The My Human tab shows Sandra's model of you — a structured summary of what she has learned about you from your conversations, notes, and learning history.

Click **Generate Profile** to have Sandra produce this summary. If no profile exists yet, she will build one from your existing memories.

You can view the full memory list that feeds into this profile by clicking **View all memories →**, which takes you to [Memories](memories.md).
