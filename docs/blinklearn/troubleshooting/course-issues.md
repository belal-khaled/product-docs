# Course & Content Issues

## A course isn't loading

- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the page
- Clear your browser cache and reload
- If using mobile, try force-closing the app and reopening

## My progress wasn't saved

Progress is saved automatically at the end of each lesson. If your progress seems lost:

- Check that you completed the lesson fully (reached the end) rather than just opening it
- Make sure you were signed in when you completed it — guest sessions do not save progress

## A lesson video isn't playing

- Check that your browser allows autoplay for blinklearn.com
- Try a different browser or device
- Disable VPN or firewall tools that may block the video CDN

## I can't find a course I enrolled in

Go to your [Library](../features/library.md) — all enrolled courses appear there. Use the filter to show **Courses**.

## Content seems outdated

Course content is managed by the author. If you believe content is incorrect or outdated, use the feedback option inside the lesson to report it to the author.
