# Chat & Sandra Issues

## Sandra isn't responding

- Check your internet connection
- Refresh the page and try again
- If the issue persists, try signing out and back in

## Sandra's answers don't seem relevant to my learning

Sandra's responses improve as your learning history grows. If you're new to BlinkLearn, she has less context to draw from. Try:

- Completing a few lessons first
- Taking notes as you learn
- Telling Sandra directly what you're focused on (e.g. "I'm studying leadership — what have I covered so far?")

## Sandra seems to have forgotten something

Sandra's memory is based on lessons completed, notes taken, and insights generated. If she seems unaware of something, check:

- That the lesson or content was fully completed (not just opened)
- That the note was saved (not left as a draft)

You can also view what Sandra knows in [Memories](../features/memories.md).

## Conversation history is missing

Conversations are tied to your account session. If you signed in on a new device, you may need to scroll up to find older threads. If threads are genuinely missing, contact support.
