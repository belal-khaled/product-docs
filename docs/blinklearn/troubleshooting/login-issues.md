# Login Issues

## Incorrect email or password

Double-check that you're using the same email and password you registered with. Passwords are case-sensitive.

Use [Forgot password?](../account/password-reset.md) on the login page to reset if needed.

## Google sign-in not working

- Make sure you are selecting the same Google account you used to register
- Try clearing your browser cache and cookies, then attempt Google sign-in again
- Disable browser extensions temporarily and retry

## Account locked or suspended

If you see a message that your account is locked, contact support. Do not attempt to create a new account with the same email.

## Signed up but can't sign in

Check your email for a verification link if you registered with email and password. The link must be clicked before your account is active.

## Shared Envisioning credentials

BlinkLearn shares identity with other Envisioning products. If you've changed your password in BlinkLife or another product, use the updated password here too.
