# Deleting Your Account

Deleting your BlinkLearn account permanently removes your profile, learning history, notes, and AI memories. This cannot be undone.

## Before you delete

- Download any notes or pages you want to keep
- Note that deleting your account may also affect your access to other Envisioning products if they share the same identity

## How to delete

1. Go to your [profile settings](profile-settings.md)
2. Scroll to the bottom of the page
3. Click **Delete account**
4. Confirm by entering your email address

Your account will be scheduled for deletion. If you change your mind, contact support within 7 days.

## Contact support

If you need help with your account or are unsure about deletion, contact the support team before proceeding.
