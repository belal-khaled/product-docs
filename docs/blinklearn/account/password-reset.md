# Password Reset

1. Go to [blinklearn.com](https://www.blinklearn.com) and click **Log in**
2. Click **Forgot password?**
3. Enter the email address on your account
4. Check your email for a reset link — it expires after 30 minutes
5. Click the link and choose a new password

## No email arrived?

- Check your spam or junk folder
- Make sure you entered the correct email address
- Allow a few minutes before requesting a second link

## Signed up with Google?

If you created your account with Google, there is no BlinkLearn password to reset. Sign in with Google and manage your password through your Google account settings.
