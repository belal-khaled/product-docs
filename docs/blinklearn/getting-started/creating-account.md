# Creating Your Account

## Sign up

Go to [blinklearn.com](https://www.blinklearn.com) and click **Get started** or **Sign up**.

You can create an account with:

- **Email and password** — enter your email, choose a password, and verify your email address
- **Google** — sign in with your existing Google account for a faster setup

## Onboarding

After creating your account, BlinkLearn walks you through a short setup:

1. **Your role** — tell us how you learn best
2. **Your companion** — choose a name and style for your AI companion
3. **Your handle** — pick a unique username for your public profile
4. **Your goals** — select the learning areas you want to focus on

This helps Sandra personalise your experience from day one.

## Shared identity across Envisioning

If you already use another Envisioning product (BlinkLife, Finerminds), your account and AI memory are shared. You can sign in to BlinkLearn with the same credentials.
